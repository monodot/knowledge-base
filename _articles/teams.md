## Teams for Firefox on Linux

Teams on Linux is a funny story, and hopefully you never have to deal with it. However, if you do:

- **Check Firefox codec support:** go to `about:support` in a Firefox tab, find _Codec Support Information_ and verify that Software 
- **Check OpenH264 Video Codec plugin:** go to `about:addons` -> Plugins -> find _OpenH264 Video Codec provided by Cisco Systems, Inc._ -> ensure it is set to **Always Activate**
